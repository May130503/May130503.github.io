Exploring course evaluations

Examining how evaluation scores relate to instructor age, language, and other course characteristics.

The question

How do instructor and course characteristics relate to student evaluation scores in the course-provided University of Texas at Austin dataset?

My approach

  • Summarized instructor ages and displayed their distribution in a histogram.
  • Examined age and evaluation scores through a scatterplot and correlation.
  • Compared evaluation scores across language groups using boxplots, histograms, group means, and a two-sample t-test.
  • Built frequency tables, bar charts, and a two-way table of instructor rank and course level.

Findings from my submitted report

The report describes a weak negative association between age and evaluation score (r ≈ −0.11). It reports average scores of about 4.1 and 3.9 for the two language groups, with a t-test p-value of approximately 0.007.

These are observational associations, not evidence that an instructor characteristic causes a change in evaluation scores. The figures above are taken from the submitted report; the source dataset was not included for independent recalculation.
